Cookie Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-17

MixForge sets no cookies

This site does not set cookies and runs no advertising or analytics trackers. That is why you don't see a cookie banner — there is nothing to consent to.

What is stored in your browser

ItemWherePurposeLifetime
mixforge_tokenlocalStorageKeeps you signed in (session token)Until logout, password reset, or 14 days
Theme preferencelocalStorageRemembers dark/light modeUntil cleared

Both are strictly functional, never sent to third parties, and you can clear them at any time from your browser's site-data settings.

Third parties

If you start a paid checkout you are redirected to Stripe, which sets its own cookies on stripe.com under Stripe's privacy policy. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which serves files without setting cookies.
