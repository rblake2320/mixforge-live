Rights & Licensing

Last updated: 2026-07-17

Who owns what

  • Your recordings and uploads stay yours. MixForge takes only the technical license needed to store, process, and play them back to you.
  • Seeded beats in the demo catalog are royalty-free demo content for trying the product.
  • Stems generated from your audio belong to you, subject to the rights you hold in the source material — separating a track you don't own does not create rights you didn't have.

Mashups and other derivatives

Combining stems — for example, vocals from one track over the beat of another — creates a derivative work. You need rights to every source that feeds a mashup, not just one of them, and separating a track never grants rights you didn't already hold in it. Mashups made on MixForge are marked private-use by default; publishing or selling a derivative is your decision and your responsibility, and a takedown against any one source applies to every derivative built from it.

Imported links

When you import a YouTube, SoundCloud, or direct audio link, our stem provider fetches it on your behalf. You are responsible for having the rights to process that material, exactly as if you had uploaded the file yourself.

DMCA takedowns

MixForge complies with the DMCA. Rights holders can submit a takedown notice; targeted content is placed under review immediately (it stops being served the moment a notice targets it) and removed if the notice is valid. Repeat infringers are terminated.

Review timeline: our designated agent decides takedowns within 3 business days of receipt. Content stays inaccessible during review, so a slow decision never extends the time infringing material is available — it only delays restoration of content that turns out to be legitimate.

To file a notice, email our designated agent at dmca@mixforge.live or submit programmatically to POST /api/dmca with: your name, contact email, a description of the copyrighted work, the target content, a good-faith statement, and an electronic signature. Policy details: GET /api/legal/dmca.

Counter-notices

If your content was removed and you believe the notice was mistaken, send a counter-notice to the same agent address with the content identifier and the basis of your rights; we restore content when the DMCA's counter-notice conditions are met.
